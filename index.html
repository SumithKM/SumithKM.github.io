E-commerce Customer Behaviour

Analysis of one year of UK online retail transactions (541,909 rows). Cleaning uncovered two large orders cancelled within minutes, which would otherwise have distorted the product ranking and monthly revenue.

Findings: £9.77M net product revenue, about 85% from the UK, and 65% of identified customers ordering more than once. Caveats are documented in the write-up.
